## Blocking items (cannot be resolved by further analysis here)

1. **Journal and timeline.** No scope-appropriate journal can be shown to meet the ≤ 72-day submission-to-final-acceptance requirement, and no JCR quartile could be verified (see `audit/journal_fit_assessment.md`). The 72-day constraint was waived by the author's impact-factor instruction in cycle 1; that waiver is recorded, not a pass. RIPR's own sampled medians are 132–141 days from submission to acceptance. Acceptance cannot be guaranteed by anything in this package.
2. **Scope fit.** The paper never observes peer review; it audits editorial-process statistics. At RIPR a desk rejection on scope is more likely than not (second adversarial review). A pre-submission enquiry to the editors, or retargeting to a scientometrics journal (formatting for it was not done), is needed.
3. **No persistent-identifier deposit** of data and code (R19). The raw Crossref and PubMed files are committed (about 850 files), but the cleaned files in `data/processed/` are gitignored and regenerated by `prepare_data.py`; a Zenodo-type deposit with hashes of decompressed content must be made by the authors.
4. **No human review** (R20). All reviews, including the two adversarial rounds and the audits, are by instances of the same model family.
5. **Author-side placeholders**: author names and affiliations, funding, competing interests, contributions, acknowledgements, corresponding author, journal-name abbreviation per Index Medicus.

## Residual scientific limits (disclosed in the manuscript)

- Dates are validated only for a self-selected Springer Nature subset; three publishers (the effective number of independent clusters), no Elsevier or IEEE.
- Conditional on acceptance; peer review itself is unobserved.
- The cohort-variance account is characterised, not identified.
- Remedies were evaluated at three origins only; L (learned forecaster) was trained on one or two cohorts without tuning.
- Contribution is incremental measurement with mostly negative hypothesis outcomes (H1 supported; H2 inconclusive; H3, H4, H5 not supported as pre-specified; H7 small journal-level effect, not robust at imprint level).
