Turnaround Forecaster

Paste a journal's received and accepted dates and get a calibrated forecast of how long the next accepted paper will have taken. The method widens the journal's own recent distribution by the amount that cohorts of papers shift from one period to the next.

τ² = 0.040 is the value learned from 774 Springer Nature, Wiley and Taylor & Francis journals (cohort-to-cohort shift in mean ln-days, after removing sampling noise). Set 0 to get the plain empirical forecast.

Forecast

Durations are capped at 480 days and floored at 1 day. A paper counts as visible at the origin if it was first online within the previous 12 months and accepted before the origin; if first_online is missing the accepted date is used. Results describe papers that were accepted: they say nothing about the chance that a submission is accepted. The intervals were validated for journals with at least 100 visible papers; fewer papers give a warning. Everything runs in this page; no data leave your browser.
